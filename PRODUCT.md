# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: the person who organises meetings for an association or collective (association board, club, residents' council, volunteer group). They create a group of people and need to find a date that works.
Secondary: group members, who only declare when they are available and whether they can attend in person or remotely.

## Product Purpose
Help an organiser find the best meeting date for a group. The organiser creates a group; each member declares their availability for candidate dates, specifying in-person or remote attendance. From those answers the organiser sees which date gathers the most people.
Success: the organiser picks a date with confidence, without email ping-pong.

## Positioning
Not just a date poll: every availability carries an attendance mode (in person / remote), so the organiser sees both how many people can come and how they will attend — the deciding input for hybrid meetings.

## Operating Context
Laravel 13 app with Livewire, Flux Pro and Tailwind v4. Authentication via Fortify (login, registration, passkeys, 2FA). French-speaking audience.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Confirmed: groups created by an administrator, availability declared by members with in-person/remote mode, best-date view for the administrator.
- Not yet built: all of the above business features; only auth and an empty dashboard exist.
- Undecided: product name (working name "Reunion", driven by APP_NAME), pricing, whether members need an account to answer.

## Product Principles
- The best date is the hero: everything leads to the moment the organiser sees it.
- Answering must take seconds for members, or the poll fails.
- In-person vs remote is first-class information, never a footnote.
- Honest by default: no invented proof, no dark patterns.
